A sealed clay-and-wax pellet packed with ground iron, salt and charcoal. Hold right-click for a second to crack it open and it burns warm for minutes, holding your core temperature up while you get back to shelter. Expensive to make and gone when it is used — an emergency, not a habit.

How it works

Hold right mouse for about a second. The pellet cracks with a hiss, one is consumed, and you are left holding a Spent Emberheart Shell. For the next six minutes or so the Emberheart feeds warmth into your core body temperature, ramping up over the first half-minute and fading out over the last tenth of its burn. Ember motes drift off you while it is lit, and a small readout above the hotbar counts down the time left.

It is a contest, not a cheat. The warmth is added on top of whatever the climate is doing to you, so in ordinary cold it will carry you back toward normal and hold you there — but a hard enough blizzard, or being soaked through in deep winter, can still out-pull it. That is deliberate. An Emberheart buys you time to reach shelter; it is not a shelter.

It will never cook you either. The effect stops contributing once you reach a ceiling just above normal body temperature, and it never pulls you back down — so warming yourself at a fire or a Great Hearth while one is burning works exactly as you would expect.

Crafting

Emberheart Powder (3×1 grid) — the grind step:

iron  ·  salt ×2  ·  charcoal powder ×2

Two accepted sources for the iron, so this is not gated behind a bloomery:

  • Iron ore nuggets ×8 — limonite, hematite or magnetite. The loose ore stones that scatter on the surface above an iron deposit are picked up by hand, no tool required, and give 2–3 nuggets each. Available from day one.
  • or iron bits ×4 — if you already run a bloomery, use smithing scrap instead and save yourself the walk.

Emberheart (2×2 grid) — the seal step:

emberheart powder  ·  fired bowl
beeswax  ·  sulfur powder
  • Fired bowl — any colour; this becomes the shell
  • Beeswax — the seal
  • Sulfur powder — quern-ground from sulfur ore

Sulfur is the real gate. It spawns only in specific deposits and is already wanted for blasting powder and acid, so every Emberheart costs you something you would rather have kept. Nothing in the chain needs metalworking — a quern and a pit kiln are the only tools involved.

Reclaiming shells

A 2×2 shapeless recipe turns four Spent Emberheart Shells back into one blue clay, and a pulverizer will crush a single shell for the same. Worth carrying home rather than dropping.

Behaviour

  • Duration — about 6 real minutes by default (6 in-game hours at default calendar speed)
  • Activation — roughly 1 second of held right-click, with a progress ring
  • Ramp — climbs to full strength over about 25 seconds rather than snapping on
  • Taper — fades out over the final 10% of the burn, with a warning message
  • No stacking — cracking a second one while the first still burns refreshes the timer. The heat never doubles.
  • Survives relog and server restart — the effect is stored against world time, so logging out, restarting the server, or stopping for the night all resume correctly with the right time remaining, and it does not burn away while the server is offline.
  • Ends on death — no free warm respawn.
  • Cannot be cracked open while swimming — a soaked pellet is the whole reason it is sealed in clay.

Configurable

An emberheart.json config file, auto-generated in ModConfig, exposes everything: burn duration, warmth per second, the temperature ceiling, ramp-up time, taper fraction, tick interval, and whether a second pellet refreshes the timer. The HUD readout and the ember particles can each be switched off.

Two warming models are included. Boost, the default, adds heat per second and lets severe weather fight back. Floor instead holds your core temperature at or above a set minimum. Switch with one config value, or in game with /emberheart mode boost|floor.

Commands

  • /emberheart status — your body temperature, which danger band it falls in, every factor currently pushing it up or down, and what the Emberheart is contributing right now. Useful on its own for understanding why you are freezing.
  • /emberheart start / stop — light or snuff one without spending an item (admin).
  • /emberheart mode boost|floor — switch models without a restart (admin).

Compatibility

  • Built for Vintage Story 1.22+
  • Universal — required on client and server, and safe for multiplayer. All warming is decided server-side.
  • No Harmony patches. Body temperature is adjusted through the public API, so this will not fight other temperature mods over the same patched methods.
  • Designed as a companion to Great Hearths — a hearth warms a room, an Emberheart warms the one person caught out in the open. Using both together is fine; warming at a hearth will not be capped or cancelled by a burning Emberheart.
